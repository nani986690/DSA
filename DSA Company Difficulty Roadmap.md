# 🚀 DSA Company Difficulty Roadmap

A practical guide to understanding **DSA interview difficulty across FAANG, Product Companies, GCCs, MNCs, FinTechs, and Startups**.

> ⚠️ Difficulty levels are approximate. The actual interview difficulty can vary by role, team, location, experience level, and hiring cycle.

---

## 🏆 Difficulty Levels

| Level       | Difficulty        | Typical DSA                     |
| ----------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 💀 Hard++   | Elite             | Medium + Hard, advanced DSA     |
| 🔴 Hard     | Very Strong       | Medium-heavy + Hard             |
| 🟠 Medium+  | Strong            | Easy + Medium + occasional Hard |
| 🟡 Medium   | Moderate          | Easy + Medium                   |
| 🟢 Medium-- | Beginner-Friendly | Easy + basic Medium             |
| 🔵 Easy--   | Basic             | Easy + fundamentals             |

# 📈 The Main Goal

Don't prepare DSA **company-by-company**.

Prepare **pattern-by-pattern**.

Your target should be:

```text
Easy
  ↓
Easy → Medium
  ↓
Medium comfortably
  ↓
Medium under time pressure
  ↓
Selected Hard
  ↓
Hard confidently
```

### 🔥 Golden Target

> **If you can consistently solve LeetCode Medium problems and handle selected Hard problems, you can seriously target a large portion of Product, GCC, FinTech, and Big Tech opportunities.**
